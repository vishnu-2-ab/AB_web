NIBP Module

Ultra-low-profile Blood Pressure OEM Module engineered for seamless integration.

Module Overview

This module utilizes a highly refined, non-invasive oscillometric technique to deliver fast, reliable, and reproducible blood pressure measurements across clinical applications.

Engineered specifically with a compact, ultra-low-profile footprint, it provides the ideal architecture for seamless original equipment manufacturer (OEM) integration. This space-saving design allows developers to effortlessly embed premium NIBP capabilities into handheld monitors, multi-parameter vital signs workstations, or specialized diagnostic devices without compromising on internal board real estate.

OEM Integration Ready: Perfect for rapid prototyping and mass-production of clinical-grade diagnostic hardware.
