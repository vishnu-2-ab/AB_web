SpO2 Module

High-performance OEM SpO2 integration module for dependable vital sign acquisition.

Module Overview

This OEM SpO₂ module is designed to deliver highly accurate and reliable pulse oximetry (SpO₂) and heart rate (HR) measurements for seamless integration into a wide range of medical and healthcare monitoring systems. Engineered for stable performance under challenging conditions such as patient movement and low peripheral perfusion, the module ensures dependable vital sign acquisition while minimizing false readings and signal interruptions.

In addition to precise numerical outputs, the module provides a continuous, real-time Photoplethysmogram (PPG) waveform. The waveform offers a clear visual representation of arterial pulsations, enabling effective assessment of signal quality and providing deeper insight into the patient’s peripheral perfusion status in real time.

Designed as a compact and integration-friendly OEM solution, the module can be easily embedded into multiparameter monitors, portable healthcare devices, wearable systems, and other patient monitoring platforms requiring high-performance SpO₂ functionality.
