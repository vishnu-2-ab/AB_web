Vital Signs Monitor

Advanced, Continuous Multi-Parameter Monitoring Designed for Precision Adult Patient Care.

Product Overview

The AppleBerry Vital Signs Monitor is a clinical-grade, multi-parameter monitoring solution engineered to deliver precise physiological diagnostics in fast-paced medical environments. Combining essential patient vitals tracking with highly automated decision support tools, this device is designed to improve clinical workflow efficiency and patient safety.

Featuring a vibrant 7" display, the AppleBerry monitor provides clinicians with an easily navigable interface to view real-time metrics, configure automated alarms, and analyze comprehensive trend reviews. Whether deployed for routine clinical rounds or dedicated continuous monitoring modes, the AppleBerry vital signs monitor ensures exceptional accuracy, enterprise-level connectivity, and proactive patient risk management.

Target Demographics & Settings: Designed specifically for Adult (A) patient profiles. It is ideal for general hospital wards, emergency triage, step-down units, and outpatient clinics requiring dependable, fast-acting clinical surveillance.

Key Features & Clinical Benefits

Advanced Multi-Parameter Tracking

Concurrently monitors and displays vital patient metrics including Non-Invasive Blood Pressure (NIBP), Pulse Oximetry (SpO2), Temperature, Perfusion Index (PI), and manual Respiration Rate.

Proprietary Fast NIBP Technology

Utilizes an advanced, inflation-based NIBP algorithm that reduces inflation cycle times, speeding up clinical workflows while drastically improving patient comfort.

Premium Excergen Thermometry

Integrated directly with a trusted Excergen temporal/forehead infrared scanning system for rapid, non-invasive temperature readings.

Own-Brand SpO2 Efficiency

Features proprietary, own-brand pulse oximetry technology optimized for reliable oxygen saturation readings.

Intelligent Clinical Decision Support

Built-in safeguards actively protect patients through automated auto-set alarm limits and proactive patient deterioration alerts.

Seamless Hospital Connectivity

Equipped with onboard Wi-Fi/WLAN to ensure clean integration with hospital data networks and electronic medical records (EMR).

Robust Local Data Storage

Features a high-capacity internal database capable of logging up to 500 comprehensive patient records with complete trend review playback functionality.
